Thank you to Lee for presenting Metagov's interoperable deliberative tools work, to Simone for presenting an initial collection of deliberation tools and processes, and to everyone who joined the discussion.

We agreed to **start with user stories**: what people need to accomplish through deliberation, without assuming a particular tool or protocol. These needs can guide our priorities and help identify where existing tools and standards fit.

Following the call, Bumblefudge shared a template and suggested simplifying it to four or five questions. Thanks for the follow-up!

To get the discussion started, I've prepared:

- A [lightweight user-story template](https://github.com/dds-wg/dds/issues/new?template=user-story.yml).
- [Compare analyses of the same contributions](https://github.com/dds-wg/dds/issues/24).
- [Continue a deliberation after changing providers](https://github.com/dds-wg/dds/issues/22).
- [Join a deliberation without registering](https://github.com/dds-wg/dds/issues/23).

The examples draw on my earlier design proposals and Bluesky/AT Protocol experiments. Those proposals were not adopted by community consensus; the stories are starting points for discussion.

At the next open call, I'd like us to discuss whether the template is useful, which stories reflect real needs, and what is missing. Before then, please comment on GitHub, add a story, or reply here with an example from your community or practice. Non-technical perspectives are welcome.

The complete meeting agenda for the next open call will be sent separately.

Please also reply with any corrections or additions to the meeting notes.
